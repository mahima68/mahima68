# Project selection for applications

Use a role-specific CV with two or three relevant projects. These are portfolio studies, not paid client work. Verify every detail and be ready to explain the work before using these bullets.

| Role | Lead project | Supporting projects | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data analyst | E-commerce profitability & forecasting | Job market intelligence; GA4/BigQuery | SQL, cleaning, reconciliation and reporting |
| Business analyst | E-commerce profitability & forecasting | GA4/BigQuery; BookNest | Business questions, assumptions, requirements and recommendations |
| Junior data scientist | Marketing budget uplift | E-commerce forecasting; job market intelligence | Baselines, split design, evaluation and uncertainty |
| Marketing analyst | Google Merchandise Store analytics | Marketing uplift; e-commerce analytics | Acquisition, funnels, incrementality and commercial interpretation |
| Digital marketing / content / SEO | BookNest | Google Merchandise Store analytics | Content, keyword intent hypotheses, copy, creative assets and measurement |

## Evidence-based CV bullets

### E-commerce analytics

- Built a Python/SQL analytics pipeline processing 1,067,371 source transaction lines, with documented exclusions and financial reconciliation, and deployed an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
- Evaluated weekly forecasts using rolling validation and a held-out test set; reported 17.1% WAPE for the validation-selected model alongside a stronger 12.2% simple baseline.
- Developed pricing and contribution-profit scenarios with explicit cost and elasticity assumptions; distinguished modeled economics from observed revenue.

### Marketing uplift

- Compared four uplift modelling approaches with random and purchase-propensity targeting on a 1.54-million-row sample of the corrected Criteo benchmark, using separate training, validation and test sets.
- Evaluated a fixed 20% targeting policy with doubly robust estimates and paired uncertainty; found improvement over random targeting but no clear advantage over likely-buyer targeting.

### Job market intelligence

- Built an API-to-SQL analytics pipeline with deduplicated postings, immutable observations, PostgreSQL/dbt models and an interactive synthetic demonstration dashboard.
- Collected two comparable India job-search samples of 409 and 413 provider IDs; documented sampling limits and added a reproducible 40-record AI-reviewed skill-mention agreement check.

### Google Merchandise Store

- Analysed acquisition, product and session-funnel questions using GA4 report evidence and separately executed BigQuery public-sample queries, with documented metric definitions and source boundaries.

### BookNest

- Created a zero-budget marketing portfolio for a fictional bookstore, including 20 keyword ideas, a 30-day content calendar, five carousels, an original blog draft and two email templates.
- Built an Excel measurement framework with clearly labelled synthetic examples and a separate actual-results log; no live campaign lift is claimed.

## Interview preparation

For each project, explain the business question, data grain, one quality problem, an alternative approach, the actual result and the next step. Explain AI-assisted work honestly and demonstrate understanding.

Business analyst applications will benefit from requirements gathering, process mapping, acceptance criteria and UAT evidence. Marketing applications will benefit from actual published work and consistently measured organic results. These are future evidence gaps; do not list them as completed achievements.
